I am a Ph.D. student at the **Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)**, Shenzhen, China. 
Before joining SUSTech, I received my M.Sc. degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from **The University of Hong Kong (HKU)** and my B.Eng. degree in Information and Communication Engineering from the **University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)**.

My research interests lie in **computer vision, event-based vision, and embodied perception**, with a particular focus on high-rate and low-latency visual perception for dynamic environments. 
I am interested in developing vision systems that combine conventional cameras with asynchronous sensing to enable accurate and efficient perception beyond the frame-rate limitation of standard RGB cameras.

My recent research focuses on **high-rate dense prediction**, including semantic segmentation and monocular depth estimation, as well as efficient perception for autonomous vehicles, drones, and robotic platforms.
